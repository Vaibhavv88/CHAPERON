# 🎯 THE PROBLEM

Starting and operating an industrial business often requires navigating a
complex ecosystem of **approvals, licences, documents, inspections,
compliance requirements and government support schemes**.

For an entrepreneur, the journey can involve:

- 🔹 Identifying which approvals are actually required
- 🔹 Understanding dependencies between approvals
- 🔹 Preparing multiple documents
- 🔹 Submitting applications to different authorities
- 🔹 Tracking application status
- 🔹 Managing inspections
- 🔹 Maintaining compliance and renewals
- 🔹 Finding relevant government schemes and incentives

### The result?

> **Complex processes → Repeated work → Delays → Missed deadlines →
> Reduced efficiency**

---

# 💡 OUR SOLUTION

## CHAPERON

**CHAPERON** is an intelligent industrial approvals and compliance
platform designed to bring the entire journey into **one guided,
transparent and structured ecosystem.**

Instead of making users navigate disconnected processes, CHAPERON
organizes the journey around the needs of three key stakeholders:

| 👨‍💼 Entrepreneur / Investor | 🏛️ Government Officer | ⚙️ Administrator |
|---|---|---|
| Discover approvals | Review applications | Manage users |
| Upload documents | Verify documents | Manage departments |
| Submit applications | Raise queries | Configure approval rules |
| Track progress | Schedule inspections | Monitor timelines |
| Manage compliance | Approve / Reject | View analytics |

---

# 🧭 ONE PLATFORM. THREE STAKEHOLDERS.

The CHAPERON landing page provides dedicated entry points for:

### 👨‍💼 Entrepreneur / Investor

Start or manage a business, discover required approvals, upload
documents, submit applications and track the complete approval journey.

### 🏛️ Government Officer

Review assigned applications, verify documents, raise queries,
schedule inspections and make approval or rejection decisions.

### ⚙️ Administrator

Manage users, departments, approval rules, officers, timelines,
validity, schemes and platform analytics.

# ✨ KEY FEATURES

## 🧩 1. Approval Discovery

Identify the approvals and permissions relevant to a particular
business or industrial activity.

---

## 📋 2. Guided Approval Journey

Transform a complicated approval process into a structured,
step-by-step workflow.

---

## 📄 3. Document Management

Organize and submit the documents required for different approval
processes.

---

## 🔍 4. Document Verification

Government officers can review submitted documents and identify
issues before proceeding with an application.

---

## 🏛️ 5. Government Officer Workflow

Provide officers with a centralized environment to:

- Review applications
- Verify documents
- Raise queries
- Manage inspections
- Record decisions

---

## ⏱️ 6. Timeline & Compliance Tracking

Track approval timelines, validity periods and important compliance
requirements to reduce the possibility of missed deadlines.

---

## 📊 7. Administrative Analytics

Administrators can monitor users, departments, approval rules,
schemes and overall platform activity.
